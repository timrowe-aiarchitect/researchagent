# Formist Studio · marketing kit (starter set)

This kit is written to Formist's brand system, sourced from the shared brand kit drive:
`formist-brand-kit-v8.2-FULL.zip`, `formist-tokens.css` (Marfa palette v4.0), `formist-marfa-manual.md`, `formist-color-usage.png`, and `formist-voice-doctrine.md`.

Focus: general studio launch and awareness. Not tied to one offer or case study.

## What is and isn't in here

This kit is scripts, briefs, and copy, not finished assets. Nothing here was rendered, filmed, or posted. Each folder tells you exactly what to do with it:

- **01-photography**: three shoot briefs a photographer or an AI image tool can execute from.
- **02-explainer-video**: one full script and storyboard for a 60 to 75 second studio explainer.
- **03-linkedin**: four ready-to-post captions.
- **04-instagram**: four ready-to-post captions with tile art direction.
- **05-higgsfield-ugc**: two prompt scripts formatted for Higgsfield CU (avatar brief, hook, script, camera direction, on-screen text). Paste these into Higgsfield to generate the actual video.

## Brand quick reference

**Positioning**: strategy, design, and AI studio for founders, C-level, and innovation leads. "Ambition, given form." "Speed is cheap. Direction isn't." "14 weeks, concept to launch."

**Voice**: direct (states, never sells), exact (every claim carries a number or a name), dry (understatement, never jokes, and only in social/Review). No em dashes. Sentence case. Active voice. Numerals in display and social ("14 weeks", not "fourteen weeks"). No superlatives unless externally awarded.

**Banned words**: solutions, leverage (as a verb), passionate, innovative, cutting-edge, seamless, empower, unlock, elevate, delve, synergy, best-in-class, world-class, holistic, robust (unless literally measured), delight. No "it's not X, it's Y" constructions. No rule-of-three stacking back to back. No paragraph opening on a rhetorical question.

**The line system** (LinkedIn and social): flag, argument, structure. One idea per post. Silence between campaigns is brand behavior, don't fill quiet weeks with filler posts.

**Color** (Marfa palette v4.0): pick one wall (Stucco `#F3F0E7`, Milled Aluminum `#C9CDCB`, Smoke `#262626`, Deep Holly `#042609`, or Faint Copper `#D2A78D`), one reader from the certified pairings, one accent. Never a fourth value on one surface.
- Field Olive `#525B2B` is the interactive accent for web and product, always.
- Brass `#785F20` carries rules and numerals in documents and decks.
- Copper `#9C5A33` and Gold `#8C845A` for premium print.
- Cadmium `#CE4A21` is "the object": one painted volume, rationed, never UI, never a chart, never an error state, never twice on a surface.
- Social: one clash color per tile, never two. Stat numerals sit in the tile's clash color. The mark is always monochrome (ink `#0A0A0A` or paper `#F4F3EF`), never in brand colors.

**Photography grade**: leans Moss. Desert light, warm shadow, no saturation war with the palette. Images stay quiet and true, color enters through surfaces and objects, not through post-processing punch.

**Type**: PP Neue Montreal. Bold for wordmark and headlines, Medium for subheads and UI, Book for body, Thin for oversized numerals only. Commercial license required before public use.

**Design laws**: no gradients, no drop shadows, no rounded corners, no em dashes. Every screen and layout must survive full desaturation with its hierarchy intact.

**Logo**: strictly monochrome. Use `Formist Logo Light.png` / `Formist Logo Dark.png` from the brand kit depending on ground. Never recolor the mark.

**Language**: English is default across social. German uses Sie on the website, proposals, and first contact; social media uses du where it reads natural, channel by channel. This starter set is English only.
