# Photography · three shoot briefs

Grade: Moss. Desert light, warm shadow. No saturation war with the palette. Shoot for quiet, true color, not for punch you add later. Every frame should survive a grayscale check with its hierarchy intact.

Format all three for a mixed set: horizontal for web and LinkedIn, a 4:5 crop for Instagram feed, a 9:16 crop for Instagram and LinkedIn stories.

---

## Brief 1 · The studio at work

**Use**: website hero alternates, LinkedIn "who we are" posts, About page.

**Subject**: the studio itself, mid-sprint. Not a posed team photo, a working room caught in motion.

**Setting**: the studio space, natural light from one side (window light, not overhead fluorescent). Walls or backdrops in Stucco or Milled Aluminum. A whiteboard or wall system showing a sprint timeline, visible but not legible in the frame (avoid real client data).

**Composition**: one wall, one reader, one accent, same rule as the color system. Let one Field Olive object (a marker cap, a folder, a chair) be the single accent color in frame. Everything else in Foundation neutrals: ink, warm gray, sand, bone.

**People**: 2 to 4 people, mid-task (sketching, reviewing a print-out, standing at a whiteboard). Candid postures, no smiling-at-camera. Hands visible, doing something specific.

**Framing**: wide establishing shot plus 2 to 3 tighter cuts (hands on paper, a face in profile reading a screen, a detail of the whiteboard).

**Avoid**: laptop-stock-photo energy, forced collaboration poses, any second clash color competing with the one accent, rounded-corner props, shiny gradients in the background (screens, glass).

---

## Brief 2 · The object

**Use**: Instagram grid anchor posts, case study lead images, the one place Cadmium is allowed to appear.

**Subject**: a single physical artifact from the studio's process, a printed prototype, a model, a bound deck, a material sample. One object, one surface, one color moment.

**Setting**: a clean desk or table in Stucco or Faint Copper. Top-down or 3/4 angle. Nothing else on the surface except the object and, optionally, one tool (a pencil, a scale ruler).

**Color rule**: this is the shot where Cadmium can appear, and only as the object itself or one element of it. Never let Cadmium and Copper share the frame. If the object isn't naturally Cadmium, don't force it. Moss and Faint Copper are the safe defaults.

**Light**: hard desert light from one side, real shadow, no fill light washing it out. The shadow is part of the composition.

**Framing**: one hero frame, then 2 macro details (texture, an edge, a stitched binding, a printed number).

**Avoid**: lifestyle staging, hands unless the object is mid-use, more than one object in frame, busy backgrounds.

---

## Brief 3 · Portraits

**Use**: team page, founder-led LinkedIn posts, case study bylines.

**Subject**: individual portraits of studio principals and team members, shot the same way so the set reads as one system.

**Setting**: same backdrop for everyone (Stucco or Smoke, pick one for the whole set), same light direction, same distance from camera. Consistency is the brand move here, not personality lighting per person.

**Framing**: head and shoulders, slight angle off dead-center, eyes to camera. One clean expression per person, direct not performative. A second frame looking off-camera, mid-thought, for use where a caption needs room to breathe.

**Wardrobe note**: no branded clothing, no logo tees. Neutral tones that won't fight the Foundation palette (avoid saturated reds, blues, or a second clash color competing with whatever accent the surface uses).

**Avoid**: corporate headshot lighting (flat, shadowless), forced smiles, busy office backgrounds, gradients or bokeh that break the "no gradients" law when the shot gets used near brand color blocks.
