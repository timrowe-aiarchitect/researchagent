# Instagram · four posts

Tile rule from the brand kit: one clash color per tile, never two. Stat numerals sit in the tile's clash color. The mark stays monochrome always, never recolored into a clash. Captions are shorter than LinkedIn and can carry the one dry line, since social captions are where dry humor is allowed.

---

## Post 1 · Feed anchor, text tile

**Tile art direction**: ground Deep Holly, text in Paper, one clash accent in Butter for a single underline beneath "given form." No photo, type only. PP Neue Montreal Bold, centered, generous margin.

**Tile copy**: "Ambition, given form."

**Caption**:

Ambition, given form.

Formist is a strategy, design, and AI studio for founders and C-level teams. We build the thing, not the deck about the thing.

#formiststudio #foundersled #designstudio

14 weeks, concept to launch.
