# Explainer video · "Ambition, given form"

Length: 65 seconds. Format: 16:9 master, cut down to 9:16 and 1:1 for social.

Voice: one voiceover artist, direct and unhurried. No upbeat ad-read energy. Think a person who is certain, reading something they wrote themselves.

Music: minimal, no swelling strings, no trap hi-hats. A single instrument or drone that stays under the voice and drops out at the CTA.

Type on screen: PP Neue Montreal, Bold for headline cards, Book for supporting lines. Numerals as numerals. Ink on Stucco for day scenes, Paper on Deep Holly for the closing card.

---

## Storyboard

**00:00 to 00:06 · Flag**
Visual: brief 2's object shot (the object, hard desert light, one shadow). Hold on it.
VO: "Most studios sell you a process."
On-screen text (card, appears at 00:04): none yet, let the image sit.

**00:06 to 00:16 · Argument**

Visual: sequence of process beats, cut fast but not frantic. A sketch becomes a wireframe becomes a built screen becomes a printed object. 4 cuts, roughly 3.5 seconds each.
VO: "A sprint starts with one question and ends with one answer, built, not proposed. No handoffs. No decks explaining decks."
On-screen text at cut 3: "No decks explaining decks." (this is the one dry line, deliver it flat, don't wink twice)

**00:30 to 00:45 · Proof**
Visual: brief 3 portrait, a principal at a desk, looking up from work, direct to camera for one beat, then back down.
VO: "14 weeks, concept to launch. Measured on the last 6 sprints, not asserted."
On-screen text: large numeral card, "14" in Thin display weight, "weeks, concept to launch" in Book underneath. Brass rule above the numeral.

**00:45 to 00:58 · The line**
Visual: return to brief 1's studio wide shot, now with people stepping back from the whiteboard, sprint visibly done.
VO: "Speed is cheap. Direction isn't."
On-screen text: the line itself, centered, Bold, alone on a Stucco card. Let it sit 3 full seconds with no other motion.

**00:58 to 01:05 · Close**
Visual: hard cut to Deep Holly card, Formist wordmark (monochrome, Paper on Deep Holly), centered.
VO: "Formist. Start a sprint."
On-screen text: "Start a sprint" as the single CTA line under the mark, Field Olive is not used here since this is print grade, not product, so the CTA sits in Paper with a Brass rule.
End card holds 2 seconds after VO ends. No end-card music sting, let it cut to silence.

## Delivery notes for the editor

- Cut hard, don't cross-dissolve. Gradients and soft transitions are off-brand at the pixel level, hold that discipline in motion too.
- No lower thirds, no corner bug, no animated logo reveal. The mark appears once, static, at the end.
- If a client testimonial line is added later, it replaces the "no decks" line at 00:16, not the proof beat at 00:30. The numeral stays load-bearing.
