# Higgsfield CU · two UGC scripts

These are prompt scripts formatted to paste into Higgsfield CU. Each has an avatar brief, a hook, a full script, camera direction, on-screen text, and a CTA. Nothing here has been generated, run these through Higgsfield to produce the actual video.

Brand compliance for AI-generated UGC: keep wardrobe and backgrounds in Foundation neutrals (ink, warm gray, sand, bone, paper), let one accent color carry the frame if the tool allows background control, and if the tool auto-adds a caption/logo overlay, set it to monochrome (ink or paper) and flag it for a manual fix if it defaults to a gradient or a rounded badge, both are off-brand.

---

## Script 1 · "The deck I didn't need"

**Avatar brief**: mid-30s to mid-40s, founder or operator archetype, calm and a little tired, not hyped. Neutral top (no logos, no bold graphic tees), plain background (home office or neutral wall, nothing branded). Delivery: direct to camera, low energy at the start, a small lift at the end, never salesy.

**Hook** (first 2 seconds, on screen and spoken): "I almost hired an agency to make me a deck about my own idea."

**Full script** (approx. 30 seconds spoken):

"I almost hired an agency to make me a deck about my own idea.

I already knew what I wanted built. I didn't need a strategy presentation telling me what I'd already told them.

So I found Formist instead. Strategy, design, and the actual build, same people, no handoff in between.

14 weeks later it was live. Not a deck. The thing.

If you already know what you want, stop paying for people to repeat it back to you."

**Camera direction**: single static shot, no zoom, no cutaways. If Higgsfield CU supports a second angle, add one slight reframe at "14 weeks later" only, hold everything else still.

**On-screen text**: burn in only two lines, timed to the pauses.
- At "I already knew what I wanted built": no text, let the line breathe.
- At "14 weeks later it was live": text card, "14 weeks" in Ink on Paper, Bold, no drop shadow, no rounded corner box.

**CTA (final 2 seconds, on screen)**: "Formist. Start a sprint."

## Script 2 · "What a sprint actually looks like"

**Avatar brief**: late 20s to mid-30s, studio-side voice (reads as someone who works there, not a customer), warm but plain-spoken, dry delivery rather than upbeat. Setting: neutral studio-adjacent background, natural light, no branded signage visible.

**Hook**: "Everyone asks what a 'sprint' actually means. Here's the honest version."

**Full script** (approx. 35 seconds spoken):

"Everyone asks what a 'sprint' actually means. Here's the honest version.

Week one, we argue about what's worth building until we agree on one answer.

Then we build it. Same team, strategy through design through the working thing, no handoffs, no waiting on another department's calendar.

14 weeks, concept to launch, measured on our last 6 sprints.

No decks explaining decks. Just the thing, on time."

**Camera direction**: static medium shot throughout. No b-roll insert, this script relies on the speaker holding attention, not on cutaways.

**On-screen text**: one card only, at "14 weeks, concept to launch", Ink on Paper, Bold, centered, holds through the next line then clears before the final line lands with no text under it.

**CTA (final 2 seconds, on screen)**: "Formist. Start a sprint."
